Quiet Cabin Morning
Shaira Sasan

Quiet Cabin Morning

Quiet Cabin Morning is a cozy 3D environment scene inspired by peaceful mornings in a forest cabin. The warm interior lighting contrasts with the cool, foggy view outside the window to create a calm and relaxing atmosphere. Small details like the workspace, books, and personal objects were added to make the space feel lived-in and comforting.

Shaira Sasan

Modern Apartment

A contemporary three-story home exterior designed through our outdoor architectural activity. This project emphasizes sleek shapes, organized balcony railings, and lifelike lighting to create a modern urban look. Created and visualized in Blender.

PermPoon
Shaira Sasan

PermPoon

This is a 3D recreation of PeRmPooN, the mascot for the PondPhuwin couple. I have recreated the character featuring its turquoise body, orange torso, and distinctive yellow headphones. The design emphasizes the heart detail on the cheek, the “PP” logo on the chest, and the “PeRmPooN” text on the back. My aim was to convey its playful spirit while showcasing it in a refined 3D style..

Polcasan

Polcasan

Polcasan is a cherished mascot associated with TayNew, and this 3D version is my fan art tribute to them. I tried to reflect the character’s gentle, rounded shapes and its sweet, appealing expression while staying true to the original mascot's design.

Skin Elixir
Shaira Sasan

Skin Elixir

Skin Elixir is a 3D visualization project aimed at examining lighting, realistic materials, and a commercial presentation style. I focused on clear reflections, accurate glass materials, and a sophisticated ambiance to emphasize the product's high-end quality. This project provided me with the opportunity to refine my skills in product staging, composition, and cinematic lighting techniques. Created, textured, and rendered using Blender.
